# Public data notices and route-geometry access

This directory contains a frozen research scenario for 13 October 2026 at 08:00 Australia/Melbourne. It is not a current journey-planning feed. The original study values and coordinates are unchanged.

Its `routes.features[].geometry` values are also supplied, without changing coordinates, in [`route_geometries.geojson`](route_geometries.geojson). That separately accessible machine-readable collection carries the ODbL notice and is the complete published route-geometry database component. ## Derivation and access method

The research used an OpenStreetMap Victoria PBF extract, cropped with osmium-tool 1.19.1 using `--bbox 144.30,-38.40,145.60,-37.40 --strategy smart --set-bounds`, plus four archived Transport Victoria GTFS feeds. OpenTripPlanner 2.9.0 built a graph from the crop and feeds with `java -Xmx4G -jar otp-shaded-2.9.0.jar --build --save <build-directory>`. It selected the first saved itinerary for each of 100 directional queries at the fixed scenario time. No manual edits to the OSM network or route coordinates were made. The project exporter copied the validated OTP polylines into `study.json`; `scripts/export_route_geometries.mjs` copies those same geometries into the ODbL-labelled collection. This describes the alteration method for any graph-derived component under ODbL section 4.6(b), while the complete published route-geometry collection itself is directly available under section 4.6(a).

The archived raw PBF, GTFS ZIPs and OTP runtime graph are retained only in the private research environment. Current downloads may differ and will not reproduce the historical study exactly. The OSM data source is available from [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright); the timetable source is the [Transport Victoria GTFS Schedule](https://opendata.transport.vic.gov.au/dataset/gtfs-schedule). The full [ODbL text](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) and [CC BY 4.0 text](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode) govern their respective components.
